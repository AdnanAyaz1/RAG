# Phase 1 — Understand the Client

# Chapter 01 — Project Kickoff

---

# Objective

The Project Kickoff is the official starting point of a software project.

Its purpose is to ensure that everyone involved understands the project before any design or development begins.

This meeting aligns the client and the development team on the project's vision, objectives, scope, timeline, and communication process.

A successful kickoff reduces misunderstandings, minimizes rework, and establishes a shared understanding of what will be delivered.

---

# Why This Phase Exists

Developers often make assumptions that later prove incorrect.

The kickoff phase exists to replace assumptions with agreed facts.

By the end of this phase, every stakeholder should understand:

- Why the project exists.
- Who the users are.
- What problem the software solves.
- What success looks like.
- What constraints exist.

---

# Activities

Typical activities include:

- Introduce stakeholders.
- Understand the client's business.
- Discuss the project vision.
- Identify the target users.
- Define business goals.
- Define the MVP objective.
- Discuss budget and timeline.
- Identify major risks.
- Decide communication channels.
- Agree on next steps.

---

# Inputs

The kickoff meeting usually starts with:

- Client idea
- Existing documentation (if any)
- Wireframes or Figma (optional)
- Budget estimate
- Timeline expectations

---

# Outputs

This phase should produce:

- Project Vision
- Business Goals
- Stakeholder List
- High-Level Scope
- Constraints
- Risks
- Communication Plan
- Action Items

These outputs will be used in the Requirements Engineering phase.

---

# Best Practices

- Listen more than you speak.
- Ask clarifying questions.
- Do not propose technical solutions too early.
- Confirm assumptions with the client.
- Record decisions.
- Capture unanswered questions.

---

# Exit Criteria

The kickoff phase is complete when:

- The business problem is understood.
- Stakeholders are identified.
- The MVP objective is agreed upon.
- Initial risks are documented.
- The team is ready to begin gathering requirements.
